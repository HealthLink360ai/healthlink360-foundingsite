How it works

From scattered data to your next best step.

HealthLink360 connects your medical history, everyday health, and daily life into one story, then helps you act on it. Here is what actually happens behind the scenes.

1

Connect your health story

Link your medical records, wearables, and daily routines. HealthLink360 draws on 400+ data points, appointments, medications, sleep, food, movement, so nothing about your health lives in its own disconnected silo.

2

Meet your Digital Twin

Your information becomes a living picture of you, a Digital Twin that updates as your life changes. Your Twin Score gives you a fast read on how the pieces are trending together.

3

Get guidance from Coach360

Coach360 turns the fuller picture into a clear, specific next step, grounded in what is actually happening in your life rather than generic advice.

"Your blood pressure is trending up. Not an emergency. Not nothing. A 10-minute walk after dinner is a good first move."
4

Understand the why

Every insight shows what HealthLink360 noticed, why it may matter, and where the information came from, so you are never just told what to do without knowing the reasoning behind it.

5

Take the next step, in your season

Whether you are getting well after something changes, building healthier habits, or protecting progress that is already working, guidance is shaped around where you are right now, not a one-size-fits-all plan.

6

Stay connected, between visits

Your care team sees the appointment. HealthLink360 helps make sure what happens after it, the choices, the setbacks, the progress, does not get lost before the next one.
