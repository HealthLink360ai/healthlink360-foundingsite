How it works

Healthcare has more data than ever.
It still only sees you in snapshots.

A wearable, a lab result, a portal, a pharmacy record, a care visit: each one sees a piece of you, and misses the rest. HealthLink360 connects them into one picture.

0/6 pieces placed
Tap each snapshot to see what it shows, and what it misses on its own

Sees: Heart rate, steps, and sleep, tracked in real time.

Misses: It knows your heart rate spiked Tuesday. It has no idea you also skipped two doses of your blood pressure medication that week.

Sees: The numbers your last panel came back with.

Misses: An A1c can look fine in isolation. It does not show the two stressful months that got you there, or the plan to change it.

Sees: Visit notes and a list of medications.

Misses: It shows what was prescribed. It has no idea whether you can afford to fill it, or whether it fits into a day that is already full.

Sees: What was filled, and when.

Misses: A late refill shows up as a data point. It does not show the ride that fell through, or the copay that had to wait until payday.

Sees: Meals, movement, and mood, logged on your own.

Misses: It sees a rough week. On its own, it never connects that week to what your last lab result was already trying to tell you.

Sees: One conversation, one point in time.

Misses: Your care team sees the appointment. On its own, that visit cannot see everything that happens in the weeks before or after it.
