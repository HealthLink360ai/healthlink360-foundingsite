Nearly 100 teams answered the call. Nine were chosen.

Phase 2 is where we prove it: turning this record into completed preventive action for the patients, care teams, and community health workers still stuck doing this work by hand.

The problem

He downloaded his health record. It made things worse.

Two hospitals told him to take the same medication two different ways. Hundreds of pages, three systems that don't talk to each other, and one patient left to reconcile it all.

Access without trust isn't empowerment. It's homework.

How it works · The trust layer

Before we summarize a single sentence, we reconcile the record and preserve the evidence behind every fact.

1
Ingest
The full export: FHIR, C-CDA, even the PDFs. Nothing left on the table.
2
Reconcile
Three fragmented charts become one record, duplicates merged and conflicts kept.
3
Score
Every fact rated for recency + consistency across sources.
4
Act
Conflicts surfaced, cited, and resolved by a person, never buried.
