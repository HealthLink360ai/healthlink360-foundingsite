The problem

The gap isn't knowing what patients need.
It's proving anything happened next.

BETWEEN VISITS

Prevention happens at home, not in the exam room

Blood pressure, food, medications, sleep, stress: the work of prevention lives in daily life. Your EHR sees the visit. It doesn't see the 361 days between annual visits.

AFTER THE REFERRAL

Referrals leave. Evidence rarely comes back

Screeners fire, care gaps close on paper, referrals go out, and follow-through disappears into phone tag, sticky notes, and systems that don't talk to yours.

AT REPORTING TIME

You can't fund what you can't show

UDS, HEDIS, grants, and value-based contracts all ask the same question: what actually changed for the patient? Without action-level evidence, the answer costs you revenue.

How it works

From fragmented history to verified prevention, in three moves

01

Build the Prevention Passport

We ingest authorized records (SMART on FHIR / EHI export) and pair them with patient-confirmed current truth: goals, food context, routines, barriers, and the people they trust. Out comes a concise baseline and a queue of real prevention opportunities.

02

Activate safe, consented support

CHWs, Food-as-Medicine programs, coaching, and community partners work one governed journey, with role integrity. AI agents act only inside Agent Readiness boundaries: required inputs, consent, exclusions, and an accountable human, declared per feature.

03

Return evidence to the chart

Every offer, decision, action, barrier, and response is tracked through the preventive measure lifecycle. Your clinicians review a concise Digital Twin Snapshot, evidence in, not another portal to check.
